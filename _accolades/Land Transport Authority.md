<center>
	<p><b>Fifth International Conference on Highway Engineering 2024 - Young Professional Award</b><br><b>Adoption of Carbon Mineralised Concrete for Precast Concrete Segments in Singapore’s North-South Corridor</b></p>
	<p>The iCHE event is an important opportunity for public and private sector entities involved in highway transportation, as well as interested members of the public, to stay updated, gain knowledge, and build networks. Several of LTA officers presented technical papers on LTA’s green initiatives, traffic management, and road maintenance practices to share Singapore’s experience in developing and managing our road network and the “Adoption of Carbon Mineralised Concrete for Precast Concrete Segments in Singapore’s North-South Corridor” paper clinched the “Young Professional Award”.</p>
	</center>
	
![](/images/ACCOLADES/LTA/Public_Service_Science__Technology___Engineering_Excellence_Award_2024.jpg)
<center>
	<p><b>Public Service Science, Technology &amp; Engineering Excellence Award 2024</b><br><b>Underpinning of NSL MRT Tunnels by Rectangular TBM and Construction of New Mined Tunnels with Ground Freezing Method at Marina Bay Station"</b></p>
	<p>The Public Service Science, Technology and Engineering Conference 2024 gathered many Public Service Science, Technology and Engineering (STE) leaders and professionals to share ideas, collaborate, and strengthen networks. Aiming to ignite innovation in Singapore’s public sector, the event also recognised seven projects (including LTA) led by the various Public Service STE agencies.</p>
	</center>
